Why it works

Starts where you are

Every topic opens with ideas from 5th and 6th grade, then builds one step at a time.

Practice by doing

Drag angles, tap points, adjust values, build proofs and type answers. Multiple choice is the exception.

Explains why

A wrong answer shows the steps and points you to the exact notes.

No repeated questions

A question appears once until you have seen the whole bank. After that, only for scheduled review.

Read-aloud support

A voice reads questions, hints and explanations aloud. You choose the voice and speed.

Friends and leagues

Add friends by username and compare XP in a weekly league. No real names needed.

Private and free

No ads, no trackers, no purchases. Coins are only for fun.
