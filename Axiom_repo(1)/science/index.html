The science behind Axiom

Every feature below is chosen because research on how people learn supports it. This page lists the papers so you can check us.

What we avoid on purpose: loot boxes, paid power-ups, guilt-trip streak penalties, ads and endless-scroll tricks. Coins are cosmetic. Research on gamification shows mixed results, so we use game ideas only to support practice.

Content quality

Topics follow public curricula and the event rules, and lessons are cross-checked against open university and Khan Academy material listed on the resources page. Every numeric question is generated from a formula and checked by a program that solves it independently. Hardest-tier questions are written at the level of the toughest items on high-school sophomore exams.
