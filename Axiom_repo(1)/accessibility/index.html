Accessibility

Axiom is for every learner. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA on every page and in the learning app.

What we do

  • Keyboard: everything works without a mouse. Drag questions have arrow-key versions; grid questions use arrow keys and Enter.
  • Screen readers: landmarks, labelled controls, live announcements for right and wrong answers, and text descriptions for pictures and simulations.
  • Voice: a built-in voice reads questions, hints and explanations. Choose the voice and speed in Settings.
  • Vision: text meets contrast ratios of 4.5:1 or better, works at 200% zoom and in light and dark themes, and never uses colour alone to show right or wrong.
  • Motion and sound: animations turn off if your device asks for reduced motion. Sounds can be switched off.
  • Touch: large tap targets, no required gestures or timing (timed tests are optional and say so).
  • Reading level: short sentences and plain words; every topic starts at a 5th–6th grade level.

Known gaps

  • Some interactive simulations are visual by nature. Each has a written description and a keyboard or typed alternative, but the experience is not identical.
  • Maths is written in plain text (for example “x² + 3x”) rather than MathML, so some screen readers may read symbols differently.

Tell us

If something is hard to use, tell us and we will fix it. To reach us, contact the coach who shared this link, or open an issue in the project’s GitHub repository.
