AI disclosure

Axiom was built with generative AI. This page explains what that means, how the material is checked, and how you can help us correct mistakes.

What was made with AI

  • The lessons, practice questions, hints and worked explanations.
  • The diagrams, avatars and other illustrations, which are original vector graphics produced with code.
  • The website, the learning app and the account system code.

The AI model used was Claude, made by Anthropic. A human directed the scope, topics and design goals.

What is not AI

  • No AI model runs while you use Axiom. The app is static code in your browser plus a small accounts service.
  • Your answers, progress and messages are never sent to an AI service.
  • The read-aloud voice is your device’s built-in text-to-speech.

How the content is checked

  • Numeric and algebraic questions are generated from formulas, and an independent program re-solves every one to confirm the stored answer.
  • Automated tests confirm that every question can be answered correctly in the app and that explanations are present.
  • Content follows public curricula, the event rules and open university and Khan Academy material.
Limits. Not every item has been reviewed line by line by a subject-matter expert. AI systems can state incorrect facts with confidence. Treat Axiom as practice, and confirm anything important with your textbook, teacher or the official event rules.

Report a problem

  • Source code, issue tracker and documentation are published in the project’s public GitHub repository.
  • Include the question text or a screenshot and the correct answer you expect. Errors are fixed and the question bank is rebuilt.
