Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026. This policy explains what Axiom collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language.

Short version: you can use Axiom as a guest and nothing leaves your device unless you choose to play an online game (see below). If you make an account we store only what is needed to save your progress and connect you with friends. We show no ads, use no trackers and never sell data.

Who runs this

Axiom is a free educational project. To reach us, contact the coach who shared this link, or open an issue in the project’s GitHub repository.

Guest mode

If you do not make an account, your progress is saved only in your own browser (local storage) on your device. We do not receive it. Clearing site data erases it. You can export a backup in Settings.

Online games (class and versus)

Playing online needs no account. When you join a game we store, for up to 3 hours: the nickname you typed, your chosen avatar, and for each question whether you answered correctly and how long it took. Nicknames are shown to the other players in that game. We do not collect real names, chat or location. Bots are always labelled “bot”. Games are deleted automatically. Questions are checked in your browser, so online games are for fun and class use, not exams.

Study times

The study-window coin bonus uses your accuracy by hour of day. That tally is kept only on your device and is not uploaded.

What we collect if you make an account

DataWhy
Email addressTo sign you in and recover your account. Never shown to other users.
UsernameSo friends can find you. Shown to your friends and in your league.
PasswordWe never see or store it. We store a salted, slow hash (PBKDF2-SHA-256 with a server-side secret) so it cannot be read back.
Google account ID and email (only if you choose “Continue with Google”)To recognise you. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or files.
Learning progress: XP, level, streak, avatar, answers history, settingsSo your progress follows you between devices and powers your lessons.
Friend links and weekly XPTo run friend lists and the weekly league.
Session cookie and anti-forgery cookieTo keep you signed in securely (30 days, then you sign in again).

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking scripts, no fingerprinting.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data for marketing.
  • We do not ask for your real name, phone number, address, school or photo.
  • We do not use your answers to train AI models.

Cookies and local storage

Only essential ones: a sign-in session cookie (__Host-sid, HttpOnly), an anti-forgery cookie (__Host-csrf) and local storage for your progress and settings. No consent banner is needed because there are no optional cookies.

Voice assistant

Reading aloud uses the speech voices built into your browser or device. The text is not sent to us. Some browsers use online voices run by the browser or device maker; their policies then apply. You can turn the voice off in Settings.

Other companies involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and database. Like any host it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Google, only if you press the Google button: the sign-in button loads from Google, which then receives standard browser data. Google privacy policy.

Children and students

Axiom is designed for students aged 13 and up. If you are under 13, please use guest mode, which collects nothing, unless a parent or teacher has given permission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it. Axiom is not a school record and is not run by a school.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Sessions expire after 30 days. Anti-abuse counters expire within hours. Deleted data may remain in the host’s protected backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete: Me → Friends → “Delete my account” erases your account, saved progress and friend links.
  • Access or correct: ask us and we will send or fix what we hold.
  • Export: Settings has a backup you can download at any time.
  • If you live in California or another region with privacy laws, you have the rights those laws give you. We do not sell or share personal information.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. We use strict browser protections (content security policy, HttpOnly cookies, anti-forgery tokens), hashed passwords, rate limits, and parameterised database queries. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, show a notice in the app.
