    Apex · Equations & inequalities

    Proof: The average of two numbers lies between them. What is the reason for “a < (a + b)/2”?

    Reason: Division property of inequality (divide by 2 > 0). This shows there is no “next” real number after a. Between any two reals there is always another.

    Quanta · Physicist’s toolkit: vectors, units, estimation

    A student derives that the period of a pendulum is T = 2π√(g/L). What quick check shows the formula is wrong?

    The correct form is T = 2π√(L/g).

    Apex · Quadratics & functions

    Find the mean of 3, 13, 15, 18, 21.

    Answer: 14. Add: 70. Divide by 5: 14.

    Quadratics and the discriminant Interactive Tier 14

    The parabola y = x² + bx + c has two sliders. Predict: which condition makes it touch the x-axis at exactly ONE point?

    One touching point means the quadratic has a double root, which happens exactly when the discriminant b² − 4c is zero. Trap: c = 0 puts one intercept at the origin but the other is at x = −b, so there are usually two.

    Quanta · Work, energy & power

    A motor does 1400 J of work in 10 seconds. What is its power?

    W
    Answer: 140 W. Power = work ÷ time = 1400 ÷ 10. = 140 W.

    Apex · Similarity

    Proof: A line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides proportionally. What is the reason for “AD·EC = AE·DB, so AD/DB = AE/EC”?

    Reason: Subtraction (AD·AE cancels) and division property. The converse is also true: if the sides are cut proportionally, the line is parallel to the third side.

    Quanta · Gravitation & orbits

    A satellite moves to a higher circular orbit. Which statement is correct?

    Slower, but with more total energy.

    Pythagorean theorem Interactive Tier 12

    A right triangle has legs a and b. Predict: if BOTH legs are doubled, what happens to the hypotenuse?

    c = √(a² + b²). Doubling both legs gives √(4a² + 4b²) = 2c, so the whole triangle just scales by 2. Trap: the AREA quadruples, but lengths only double.

    Apex · Transformations

    What is the smallest positive angle of rotational symmetry (degrees) for a regular 6-gon?

    Answer: 60. 360° ÷ n. 360 ÷ 6 = 60.

    Quanta · Heat & thermodynamics

    A gas at constant pressure has volume 2 L at 348 K. What is its volume at 696 K? (litres, 2 decimals)

    L
    Answer: 4 L. V/T is constant: V₂ = V₁ × T₂/T₁. = 2 × 2 = 4 L.

    Why must you check solutions after solving log₃(x) + log₃(x − 8) = 2?

    Levers and torque Interactive Tier 12

    A heavy load sits near a lever’s pivot. You can only push with a small force. Predict: where should you push to balance it?

    Balance needs force × distance equal on both sides. A small force needs a long distance. Trap: forces alone do not balance a lever, the turning effect (force × distance) does.

    Quanta · Magnetism & induction

    An ideal transformer steps 120 V down to 24 V and delivers 1 A. What primary current flows (A, 3 decimals)?

    A
    Answer: 0.2 A. Input power = output power: V₁I₁ = V₂I₂. I₁ = 24×1/120 = 0.200 A.

    Apex · Polar & parametric

    Find the modulus |3 + 6i|. (2 decimals)

    Answer: 6.71. |z| = √(3² + 6²) = √45. ≈ 6.71.

    Quanta · Quantum & nuclear physics

    A sample has half-life 5 days. How many of its 1600 nuclei remain after 15 days (assume exact halvings)?

    Answer: 200.0. 15 days = 3 half-lives. N = 1600/2^3 = 200.

    Projectile range Interactive Tier 16

    A ball is launched from the ground at a fixed speed. Ignore air. Predict: which launch angle sends it farthest across level ground?

    Range = v² sin 2θ ÷ g, which is biggest when sin 2θ = 1, at θ = 45°. Trap: 90° goes highest but not far; 30° and 60° travel the same distance as each other.
