    Quanta · Circular motion & gravity

    Find the centripetal acceleration of an object moving at 5 m/s on a circle of radius 2 m. (3 decimals)

    m/s²
    Answer: 12.5 m/s². a = v²/r = 5²/2. = 12.500 m/s².

    Quanta · Waves & sound

    Two tuning forks sound at 442 Hz and 444 Hz. How many beats per second do you hear?

    Hz
    Answer: 2 Hz. Beat frequency = |f₁ − f₂|. = 2 Hz.

    Apex · Logarithms

    Simplify log base 3 of 9 plus log base 3 of 3.

    Answer: 3.0. Add the logs: log(ab) = log a + log b. 2 + 1 = 3.

    Quanta · Electric potential & Gauss’s law

    How much energy does a charge of 6 µC gain moving through a potential difference of 140 V? Answer in millijoules.

    mJ
    Answer: 0.84 mJ. ΔU = qΔV = 6×10⁻⁶ × 140 = 0.00084 J. = 0.84 mJ.

    Apex · Series & parametric

    Find the sum of the geometric series 6 + 3 + 3/2 + …

    Answer: 12.0. Ratio r = 1/2, and |r| < 1. S = a/(1 − r) = 6/1/2 = 12.

    Quanta · Quantum & nuclear physics

    A sample has half-life 5 days. How many of its 1600 nuclei remain after 15 days (assume exact halvings)?

    Answer: 200.0. 15 days = 3 half-lives. N = 1600/2^3 = 200.
