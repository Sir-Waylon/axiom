    Privacy Policy

    Effective November 21, 2026. This policy explains what Axiom collects, why, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language.

    Short version: you sign in to use Axiom. We store only what is needed to save your progress and connect you with friends, and the sign-in ends when you close your browser. If the server cannot be reached, the trainers still open and keep your progress on your device. We show no ads, use no trackers and never sell data.

    Who runs this

    Axiom is a free educational project. To reach us, contact the coach who shared this link, or open an issue in the project’s GitHub repository.

    Signing in

    You sign in each time you open Axiom, and your progress is saved to your account so it follows you between devices. If the server cannot be reached, the trainers still open and keep your progress on your own device until you can sign in again. That on-device copy lives in your browser’s local storage; we do not receive it, clearing site data erases it, and you can export a backup in Settings.

    Skyvolt and Axiom share one account

    Skyvolt (Voltaic, Stratus) and Axiom (Apex, Quanta) are sister sites from the same project. They use one account database, so one email and password works on both. Your XP from each trainer is added together to show a combined Voltaxis level. Each site keeps its own sign-in cookie, so you sign in on each. Nothing else is shared between them.

    Shop and coins

    Coins are earned by studying and only unlock cosmetic looks. There is no real-money purchase, no loot box you can pay for, and the odds of every chest are published in the app. Your coin balance and what you own are saved with your progress.

    Online games (class and versus)

    Online games need you to be signed in. When you join a game we store, for up to 3 hours: the nickname you typed, your chosen avatar, and for each question whether you answered correctly and how long it took. Nicknames are shown to the other players in that game. We do not collect real names, chat or location. Bots are always labelled “bot”. Games are deleted automatically. Questions are checked in your browser, so online games are for fun and class use, not exams.

    Reminder emails (optional)

    Off by default. If you turn it on in Friends, we store the hour and time zone you choose and a random unsubscribe code, and send at most one email a day to your account address, only when you have a streak and have not studied yet. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Deleting your account removes these settings.

    Study times

    The study-window coin bonus uses your accuracy by hour of day. That tally is kept only on your device and is not uploaded.

    What we collect if you make an account

    DataWhy
    Email addressTo sign you in and recover your account. Never shown to other users.
    UsernameSo friends can find you. Shown to your friends and in your league.
    PasswordWe never see or store it. We store a salted, slow hash (PBKDF2-SHA-256 with a server-side secret) so it cannot be read back.
    Google account ID and email (only if you choose “Continue with Google”)To recognise you. We do not receive your Google password, contacts or files.
    Learning progress: XP, level, streak, avatar, answers history, settingsSo your progress follows you between devices and powers your lessons.
    Friend links and weekly XPTo run friend lists and the weekly league.
    Letters typed into “Add a friend”After 3 or more letters, they are sent to suggest matching usernames (“Did you mean”). They are not stored. While you are signed in and the page is open, the app also checks about every 10 seconds for new friend requests.
    Anti-abuse countersTo slow password guessing and spam. They hold only a one-way hash of your network address or login name with a count, never the address, email or name itself. We cannot read them back.
    One-time email codes (only when email verification or sign-in codes are on)To confirm your email and, if you opt in, to add a second step at sign-in. We store only a one-way hash of the 6-digit code, for 10 minutes or until used, plus a count of tries. We keep whether your email is confirmed and whether sign-in codes are on.
    Bot-check result (only when the CAPTCHA is on)When you sign up or sign in, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person. Your browser sends Cloudflare standard browser and network data; we receive only a pass or fail. We store nothing from it.
    Browser security reportsIf your browser reports that it blocked something on our pages, the report (page address and the blocked item) goes to our server and is written to a short-lived log. It carries no account data.
    Progress cookie and device report (only if you turn them on in Settings)To greet you on return and to help fix bugs on slow devices. See Cookies below. Both are optional and off by default.
    Session cookie and anti-forgery cookieTo keep you signed in securely. The session ends when you close your browser, or after 8 hours, and you sign in again next time.

    What we do not do

    • No advertising, no ad networks, no analytics or tracking scripts, no fingerprinting.
    • We never sell, rent or share your data for marketing.
    • We do not ask for your real name, phone number, address, school or photo.
    • We do not use your answers to train AI models.
    • Your data is not end-to-end encrypted: it is protected in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our host, and the people who run the service could technically read it. Please do not put anything sensitive in a username or nickname.

    Cookies and local storage

    Essential: a sign-in session cookie (__Host-sid, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict), an anti-forgery cookie (__Host-csrf) and local storage for your progress and settings. These are needed to run the service, so they are set without asking.

    Optional, off by default, in Me → Settings → Cookies and diagnostics:

    • Progress cookie (vx_pg, first-party, Secure, SameSite=Lax, 180 days): a small summary (trainer, level, XP, number of answers, date) so the home page can say “Welcome back”. It is never sent to our server by us; your browser attaches it to ordinary page requests to this site. Turning the option off deletes it.
    • Device check and error details: if you turn this on, the app reads standard browser values (number of processor cores, approximate memory, screen size, network type, graphics renderer name if your browser shares it) and runs a one-second speed test, and it keeps the last few error messages in local storage on your device. Nothing is sent unless you press “Send to developer”. A report contains that device information and error text, never your answers, name or email. We log it briefly to fix bugs and do not store it in the account database; it is limited to 6 reports an hour. Clear removes the saved errors.

    Voice assistant

    Reading aloud uses the speech voices built into your browser or device. The text is not sent to us. Some browsers use online voices run by the browser or device maker; their policies then apply. You can turn the voice off in Settings.

    Other companies involved

    • Cloudflare hosts the site and database. Like any host it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and block abuse. Cloudflare privacy policy.
    • Email delivery service (Mailtrap), only when email verification or sign-in codes are switched on: we send your email address and the code message to it so it can deliver the email. It acts for us as a processor. Accounts made before this was switched on are treated as confirmed.
    • Cloudflare Turnstile (bot check), only on the sign-up and sign-in screens when turned on. It runs in a Cloudflare frame and may set a short-lived token in your browser.
    • Have I Been Pwned: when you choose a password, the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 hash are sent to this service to check whether the password appears in known breaches. The password itself and your account details are never sent. If the service is down, sign-up continues.
    • Google, only if you press the Google button: the sign-in button loads from Google, which then receives standard browser data. Google privacy policy.

    Children and students

    Axiom is designed for students aged 13 and up. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or teacher for permission before creating an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent; if you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it. Axiom is not a school record and is not run by a school.

    How long we keep data

    Account data is kept until you delete your account. Sign-in sessions end when you close your browser or after 8 hours, whichever comes first. One-time email codes last 10 minutes. Accounts whose email is never confirmed are deleted after 7 days. Anti-abuse counters hold only hashes and are reset every time their short window ends. Online game data is deleted within hours of the game ending. Deleted data may remain in the host’s protected backups for a short time before it is overwritten.

    Your choices and rights

    • Delete: Me → Friends → “Delete my account” erases your account, saved progress and friend links.
    • Access or correct: ask us and we will send or fix what we hold.
    • Teachers and parents: a coach can ask us to delete a student’s account, and a parent can ask for the same for their child.
    • Export: Settings has a backup you can download at any time.
    • If you are in the UK or European Economic Area (GDPR): you have the right to access, correct, erase and export your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, to withdraw consent at any time (for example by turning off reminder emails or deleting your account), and to complain to your local data-protection authority. We use your data to run the account you asked for (contract), to keep the service secure (legitimate interests), and, for optional reminder emails, because you agreed (consent). We make no automated decisions with legal effect. Ask using the contact below; we aim to answer within one month. Our hosting providers may process data outside your country; they use standard contractual clauses or similar safeguards.
    • If you live in California or another region with privacy laws, you have the rights those laws give you. We do not sell or share personal information.

    Security

    All traffic uses HTTPS (TLS). We use strict browser protections (content security policy, HttpOnly SameSite cookies, anti-forgery tokens), long unique passwords checked against known breaches and stored only as slow salted hashes, email confirmation, optional sign-in codes, a bot check, rate limits, and parameterised database queries. We do not handle payment-card data, because nothing on Axiom can be bought. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

    If something goes wrong

    If we learn that account data was exposed, we will tell affected people and explain what to do, as the law requires, and where GDPR applies we will notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours when required. See also our AI disclosure for how AI was used to build the trainers.

    Changes

    If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, show a notice in the app.
